# Heart waves

The electricity of the heart travels like a wave in an excitable medium. Fire cells with the mouse, paint scars,
break a wave into spirals, defibrillate, and try **resonant feedback pacing**
([Roshan & Majumder 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12672457/)) to stop a figure-8 re-entry with weak, well-timed pulses.

Aliev–Panfilov model on a 160 × 160 sheet, simulated live in your browser (three.js, no server).
Dataset: [Ethgar/cardiac-ep-2d](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Ethgar/cardiac-ep-2d) ·
Model: [Ethgar/walrus-heart](https://huggingface.co/Ethgar/walrus-heart) ·
Code: [github.com/edgarmoreaualix/heart-waves](https://github.com/edgarmoreaualix/heart-waves)
